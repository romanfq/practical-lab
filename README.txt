Erin's Practical Lab
AQA GCSE separate sciences (Higher tier) required-practical revision game.

How to play: keep index.html and the img folder together and open index.html in a web browser,
or visit the published page. Pick a subject on the first screen. Progress is saved in that browser only.
Physics and Biology have a Paper 1 / Paper 2 / Both papers filter on their topic map.

img/<subject>.js holds the cropped question-paper and mark-scheme images for that subject.

Questions and mark schemes are from AQA past papers, as downloaded from physicsandmathstutor.com:
June 2018 to June 2025 and the Specimen.
  Chemistry (8462): Paper 1 Higher.
  Physics (8463):   Papers 1 and 2 Higher.
  Biology (8461):   Papers 1 and 2 Higher.
