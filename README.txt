Erin's Practical Lab
AQA GCSE Chemistry Paper 1 (Higher) required-practical revision game.

How to play: unzip the folder, keep index.html and the img folder together,
and double-click index.html to open it in a web browser.
Progress is saved in that browser only.

Questions and mark schemes are from AQA past papers (June 2018 to June 2025
and the Specimen), as downloaded from physicsandmathstutor.com.
